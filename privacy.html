Task Solver - Privacy Policy

Publisher: Simeon's Apps · Last updated: 15 October 2026

Task Solver is a Windows app that helps students with programming exercises: it reads a task you show it, writes the code into the project open in your editor, explains errors and makes study pages. This page explains what happens to your data. In short: the publisher does not collect anything. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

What is sent to the AI (Anthropic)

When you use a feature, Task Solver sends the material for that request directly from your PC to Anthropic's Claude API, using your own API key:

  • screenshots or the part of the screen you select, Word / PDF / PowerPoint files you choose or have open;
  • the names and contents of code files in the project you have open, when needed to place or fix the code;
  • error messages you copied or that your editor lists, and the related code (Explain an error);
  • notes you type for the AI;
  • for study mode in a browser: the address, text and a screenshot of the page in front, or screenshots of the slides.

This happens only when you press a Task Solver hotkey or button. Anthropic processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). The data does not pass through the publisher, and the publisher cannot see it.

If you use the optional offline model (Ollama), the task stays on your PC and nothing is sent.

What is stored on your PC

  • Settings, including your Claude API key, which is encrypted for your Windows account only.
  • Your license and a device code. The device code is calculated on your PC from its hardware and is never sent anywhere by the app; you share it yourself if you ask for a license.
  • History: the last 200 tasks with their result, and the screenshots / documents of each task for 30 days so it can be solved again. You can clear it any time (History › Clear history).
  • Backups of files before Task Solver changes them, logs of what it did (kept 14 days), and the study pages you create (Documents › TaskSolver › Study).

All of this stays on your PC (in %APPDATA%\TaskSolver and your Documents folder) and is removed or kept as you choose.

Other internet access

  • Study mode can download a file you have open in your browser when it is publicly available at that link (for example a PDF, or a Google Slides presentation shared with "anyone with the link").
  • No other data is sent anywhere.

Reporting an answer

"Report this answer" opens your own e-mail app with a message to the publisher containing the answer you are reporting. Nothing is sent unless you send that e-mail yourself. Reports are only used to fix problems.

Children

Task Solver is meant for students taking programming courses and is not directed at children under 13.
